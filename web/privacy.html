Privacy Policy

PhraseShelf · Last updated November 16, 2026

In short: PhraseShelf does not collect, sell or share any personal data. Your tabs and buttons are stored only on your own device, and they are never sent to us or anyone else.

What PhraseShelf stores

The tabs, buttons and text you create, plus your theme choice. These are stored locally:

  • Windows and Linux app: in a file in your user profile.
  • Android app: in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • Web app (phraseshelf.app): in your browser's local storage on that device.

Uninstalling the app or clearing the site's data deletes them. We have no copy and cannot recover them, so use Export all… to keep a backup.

What we collect

Nothing. PhraseShelf has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no crash reporting.

Clipboard

PhraseShelf writes to your clipboard only when you tap or click a button. It does not read your clipboard.

Files you import or export

Import reads only the file you choose. Export saves a file only where you choose. Both happen entirely on your device.

Network connections

  • Web app: the site is served by Cloudflare. Like any website host, Cloudflare processes basic request information (such as your IP address) to deliver the page and protect it from abuse. See Cloudflare's privacy policy. Your PhraseShelf data is never part of those requests.
  • Windows and Linux app: checks GitHub for a newer version when it starts, or when you click the version number. This request contains no personal data. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Android app: makes no network connections of its own. Updates come from wherever you installed it.

Children

PhraseShelf collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Email privacy@phraseshelf.app.
